About

Fulcrum AI exists because most AI implementation fails quietly.

Teams get access to powerful tools and nothing changes. We fix the implementation layer — the part between "we have AI access" and "AI is part of how we work."

What we believe

AI adoption fails not because the tools are bad, but because nobody owns implementation. There's no structure, no workflow design, and no training that sticks. Teams end up with a subscription they barely use.

We think the implementation layer deserves real attention — practical workflow design, direct team training, and handoff documentation that actually gets used.

Who we work with

Founder-led startups, lean agencies, and small technical teams — typically 3 to 50 people — who have already decided AI matters and now need someone to make it real.

Our approach

  • Diagnose before prescribing
  • Design workflows, not just prompts
  • Train teams directly, not just leaders
  • Document everything for long-term adoption
  • Measure practical output, not demos

Tools we work with

  • Claude and Claude Code
  • ChatGPT Team and Codex
  • Agentic workflow frameworks
  • Your existing stack and tooling
