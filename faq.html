FAQ

Common questions about working with Fulcrum AI.

Do we need to be technical to work with you?

No. We handle the setup and configuration. You just need to have a real workflow problem and a team willing to adopt new tools.

How long does implementation take?

The Setup Sprint takes 5–7 business days. The Team Enablement Package runs 2–4 weeks. Custom buildouts vary by scope.

Which AI tools do you work with?

Primarily Claude and Claude Code. We also work with ChatGPT Team and other agentic tools depending on your existing stack.

What if AI adoption doesn't stick after you leave?

We build workflows your team can follow without us — clear documentation, training recordings, and internal standards. Adoption is a core part of the deliverable, not an afterthought.

Do you offer ongoing support after the project?

Yes. The Team Enablement Package includes ongoing optimization support. For other tiers, we offer separate retainer arrangements.

What size team do you typically work with?

We work best with teams of 3–50 people. Founder-led startups, lean agencies, and small technical teams are our sweet spot.

What does the discovery call actually cover?

Your current tools, where AI adoption has stalled, your team structure, and whether there's a practical implementation fit. It's 20 minutes and we come prepared.

Can you work with enterprise companies?

Our Custom AI Operations tier is built for more complex organizations. Book a call and we'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.

Have a question that's not here?
