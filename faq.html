FAQ

Common questions about working with Novelle AI.

AI Workflow & Operations

Do we need to be technical to work with you?

No. You just need a real workflow problem and a team willing to adopt new tools. We handle all the setup, configuration, and training from there.

Which AI tools do you work with?

We use the best AI platforms available and match the right tools to your situation. You don't need to know what any of them are. We figure that out and handle all the setup.

How long does AI implementation take?

The Quick Start takes 3–5 days. The Workflow Sprint takes 5–7 business days. The Team Enablement Package runs 2–4 weeks. Custom buildouts vary by scope.

What if AI adoption doesn't stick after you leave?

We build workflows your team can follow without us. Clear documentation, role-based standards, and training recordings. Adoption is a core part of what we deliver, not an afterthought.

What size businesses do you typically work with?

We work best with professional services firms and SMBs with 2–75 people. Solo practitioners, lean agencies, and boutique professional firms are a sweet spot.

How do businesses justify the investment?

A team of 5 spending 2 hours each per week on tasks AI handles is ~40 hours a month. At a loaded cost of $75–$100/hr, that's $3,000–$4,000 recovered monthly. Most engagements pay back within weeks.

Growth, Lead Gen & Websites

What's included in a website build?

Custom design, full development, mobile-first layout, SEO foundation, Google Analytics setup, lead capture forms, and copywriting guidance. You own everything we build.

How do Meta ads work with your service?

We set up your Meta Business Manager, install the Pixel, research your audience, create the ad creative and copy, build 2–3 campaign variants, and launch. You start generating leads without having to manage any of the complexity yourself.

Do I need an existing website to run Meta ads?

Not necessarily. We can send traffic to a landing page or lead form. That said, a professional website significantly improves conversion rates. If you need both, the Growth Accelerator package bundles them together at better value.

What ad budget do I need for Meta campaigns?

We recommend a minimum of $500–$1,000/month in ad spend to gather meaningful data. Our fees cover setup and management. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and is separate from what you pay us.

How quickly can ads go live?

After kickoff, ads are typically live within 5–10 business days. That covers audience research, creative production, campaign structure, and Meta's review process.

Can I run both tracks (AI ops and growth) at the same time?

Yes, and many clients do. Running both creates compound leverage: your team works more efficiently internally while your pipeline grows externally. We'll scope them to fit your timeline and budget.

General

What does the discovery call actually cover?

Your current situation, the specific bottlenecks or growth gaps you're facing, which track and tier fits, and an honest read on whether we're the right team for it. 30 minutes and we come prepared.

Do you offer ongoing support after a project?

Yes. The Team Enablement Package includes ongoing optimization support. Growth clients can move to a monthly retainer. Other tiers have separate retainer arrangements available.

What if I'm not ready to commit to a full package?

Start with the AI Quick Start at $1,000 or the Lead Gen Setup. Both are scoped to deliver fast, tangible value with no pressure to continue. Most clients that start small expand quickly once they see results.

Do you work with enterprise or larger companies?

Our Custom AI Operations tier serves more complex organizations. For enterprise growth campaigns, reach out and we'll scope appropriately. We'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.
