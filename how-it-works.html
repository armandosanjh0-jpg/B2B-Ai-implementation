How It Works

A straightforward process from discovery to implementation.

We don't do vague AI strategy decks. We do practical setup, team training, and real workflows — delivered fast.

01

Discovery call

20 minutes. We learn about your team, your current setup, and where adoption is breaking down. We'll tell you honestly if there's a fit.

02

Workflow audit

We map your existing processes and identify 2–5 high-value areas where AI can create immediate operational leverage.

03

Tool setup and configuration

We install and configure Claude or other agentic tools for your specific environment, roles, and use cases.

04

Workflow design

We design practical, repeatable workflows your team can actually follow — not just prompts, but end-to-end processes with clear inputs and outputs.

05

Team onboarding

We train your team directly — live sessions, documentation, and usage standards so everyone knows how to use the tools effectively.

06

Handoff and support

You get full documentation, recorded sessions if applicable, and follow-up support depending on your package tier.
