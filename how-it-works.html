How It Works

From discovery call to live results, fast.

No drawn-out timelines, no strategy decks without execution. We audit, build, and launch. Whether that's AI workflows for your team or a full growth campaign for your business.

⚡

AI Workflow & Operations

First workflows live in 5–7 business days. Full team adoption in 2–4 weeks.

🚀

Growth & Digital Visibility

Websites launched in 1–2 weeks. Ad campaigns live within days of kickoff.

01

Discovery Call (30 min, free)

We map your current setup, surface your highest-value opportunities (internal workflow bottlenecks or external growth gaps), and tell you honestly which track and tier fits your situation. No pitch deck, no pressure.

02

Audit & Strategy

For operations: we map your existing processes and identify 2–5 workflows where AI creates immediate leverage. For growth: we audit your online presence, competitive landscape, and target audience to define your campaign and website strategy.

03

Setup & Configuration

We install and configure the right AI tools for your environment. For growth clients, we set up Meta Business Manager, Pixel tracking, CRM connections, and web infrastructure.

04

Build & Launch

For operations clients, we build practical, repeatable workflows your team can actually follow, with clear inputs, outputs, and role-specific guidance. For growth clients, we build your website and launch your ad campaigns with tested creative and targeting.

05

Training & Handoff

We train your team directly with live sessions, documentation, and recorded materials so everyone knows how to use the tools or manage the campaigns going forward.

06

Optimization & Ongoing Support

You get full documentation, post-launch support, and ongoing management options for growth clients. Higher tiers include dedicated follow-up and refinement so results keep building over time.

Most clients go from call to first deliverable in under two weeks.

The discovery call is free, 30 minutes, and gives you a clear picture of what's possible regardless of whether you move forward.
