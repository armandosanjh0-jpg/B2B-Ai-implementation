# Novelle — Go-To-Market & Marketing Plan

A practical plan to land your **first 3–5 paying clients in 90 days** with zero ad
budget, then build a repeatable lead engine. Written for a new, founder-led AI
implementation agency with no case studies yet.

---

## 1. The core challenge (be honest about it)

You're selling a service most buyers **don't yet know they need**, with **no
track record**. So the strategy is not "wait for inbound." It's:

> **Outbound + proof-building first. Inbound + content second.**

Your first 5 clients come from *you reaching out*, not from SEO. The website's job
right now is to make outreach convert — it's the destination, not the engine.

---

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Don't market to "businesses." Pick a wedge. Tighter ICP = easier outreach,
sharper copy, faster referrals.

**Firmographics**
- Team size: **5–50 people** (big enough to feel workflow pain, small enough to
  buy fast without procurement)
- Founder-led or owner-operated — one decision-maker
- Already paying for AI tools but underusing them (key signal)

**Best-fit niches (pick 1–2 to start):**
| Niche | Why they're a fit | Where to find them |
|-------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Marketing / creative agencies | Repetitive deliverables, margin pressure, AI-curious | LinkedIn, agency directories, Clutch |
| Professional services (legal, accounting, consulting) | Document-heavy, high billable value of time saved | LinkedIn, local chambers of commerce |
| SaaS / tech startups (seed–Series A) | Engineering + ops both want AI, no internal owner | LinkedIn, local startup hubs, Indie Hackers |
| Recruiting / staffing firms | Massive volume of outreach + screening | LinkedIn, ApolloHQ |

**Disqualifiers (your site already states these — good):**
no defined use case, no budget intent, no internal owner, "just curious."

**Trigger events that mean "reach out now":**
- They just posted a job for an "AI" or "automation" role
- Leadership posted on LinkedIn about AI/efficiency
- They recently raised funding or announced growth
- They're hiring fast (scaling pain = workflow pain)

## 3. Positioning & messaging

**One-liner:** *"We make AI part of how your team actually works — installed,
customized, and adopted in weeks, not someday."*

**The wedge insight (your whole pitch):**
> Most teams have AI *access*. Almost none have AI *adoption*. The gap isn't the
> tools — it's implementation: workflow design, setup, and training that sticks.

**Message by buyer pain:**
- *"Your team has Claude/ChatGPT but barely uses it."* → adoption problem
- *"Everyone prompts differently and outputs are inconsistent."* → standards problem
- *"You know AI should save time but nobody owns making it real."* → ownership problem

**Proof you can use before you have case studies:**
- Your own workflows (dogfood — show how *you* run the agency on Claude)
- A free mini-audit as the offer (de-risks the buyer)
- "Founding client" framing (see §7)

---

## 4. Offer ladder

Lead with a **low-friction entry offer** to build proof, then upsell.

1. **Free AI Workflow Teardown (lead magnet / hook)** — 30-min call + a 1-page
   written "here are your 3 highest-ROI AI workflows" doc. Free. Builds trust +
   pipeline. This is your outreach call-to-action.
2. **AI Setup & Training Sprint — from $1,000** (entry, fast yes)
3. **AI Workflow Setup Sprint — $2,500–$5,000** (core offer)
4. **Team Enablement Package — $5,000–$10,000** (best margin)
5. **Custom AI Operations — $10,000+** (later, once you have proof)

> The free teardown → paid sprint path is the single most important conversion
> mechanic for a new agency. It turns "trust me" into "let me show you."

## 5. Lead-generation channels (ranked for a new agency)

### Tier 1 — Do these now (fastest to first revenue)

**A. Direct outbound — cold email + LinkedIn (your #1 channel)**
- Build a list of 200 ICP companies. Tools: LinkedIn Sales Navigator (free trial),
  Apollo.io (free tier), or manual research. Get the founder/owner's name + email.
- Email finders: Apollo, Hunter.io, or just `firstname@domain`.
- Cadence per prospect: Email → LinkedIn connect → follow-up email (day 3) →
  value-add follow-up (day 7) → break-up (day 12). (Templates in §8.)
- Target: **20 personalized touches/day**. Expect ~1–3% reply→call. 200 contacts
  → ~10–20 replies → ~5 calls → 1–2 clients.
- **Keep it personalized** — reference their site, a recent post, or a specific
  workflow. Generic = ignored.

**B. Warm network + referrals (highest close rate, do day 1)**
- List everyone you know who runs or works at a 5–50 person business.
- Soft ask: *"I just launched an AI implementation agency — do you know anyone
  whose team has AI tools but isn't really using them? Happy to do a free teardown."*
- After every project, ask for 2 referrals. Referrals close 3–5× better than cold.

**C. Founding-client offer (see §7)** — discount in exchange for a testimonial +
case study. Removes the "no proof" objection.

### Tier 2 — Build in parallel (compounds over 60–90 days)

**D. LinkedIn content (your owned-media engine)**
- Post **3–5×/week** from your personal profile (people buy from people, not logos).
- Content pillars:
  1. *Teardowns* — "Here's a workflow I'd build for a [niche] team" (most shareable)
  2. *Before/after* — time saved, output quality (use your own + client results)
  3. *Myth-busting* — "AI adoption fails because of X, not the tool"
  4. *Behind the scenes* — how you run Novelle on Claude (proof you practice it)
- Every post ends with a soft CTA to the free teardown.
- Engage 20 min/day on ICP prospects' posts *before* you DM them (warms them up).

**E. Niche communities**
- Join 3–5 Slack/Discord/forums where your ICP hangs out (agency owner groups,
  startup communities, indie hacker / SaaS founder spaces, local biz groups).
- Be useful for 2–3 weeks before promoting. Answer "how do I get my team to use
  AI" questions — that's literally your offer.

**F. Partnerships / referral channels (force multiplier)**
- Partner with adjacent service providers who serve your ICP but don't do AI:
  fractional COOs, ops consultants, bookkeepers, web/dev agencies, MSPs/IT firms,
  business coaches. Offer a **referral fee (10–15%)** or reciprocal referrals.
- One good partner can send steady leads.

### Tier 3 — Later (once you have proof + cash)

**G. SEO / content site** — blog posts targeting "how to [task] with Claude",
"AI for [niche]". Slow but compounds. The site is already SEO-ready (meta,
sitemap, fast).
**H. Webinars / workshops** — free "AI for [niche]" workshop → pipeline.
**I. Paid ads** — only after you know your conversion math from organic. LinkedIn
ads + Google search ("AI consultant for [niche]") once you can afford to test.

## 6. 90-day launch playbook

### Weeks 1–2 — Foundation
- [ ] Finish site launch (set Web3Forms key, pick domain, confirm Calendly 30-min event is live).
- [ ] Pick 1–2 niches from §2.
- [ ] Build a list of 200 ICP prospects (spreadsheet: company, name, email, LinkedIn, trigger).
- [ ] Set up a simple CRM (Notion, Airtable, or a Google Sheet) with a pipeline:
      Prospect → Contacted → Replied → Call booked → Proposal → Won/Lost.
- [ ] Optimize your personal LinkedIn (headline = your offer, banner, "featured" link to site).
- [ ] Write your 5 outreach templates + 5 first LinkedIn posts.

### Weeks 3–6 — Outreach sprint
- [ ] 20 personalized outbound touches/day (email + LinkedIn).
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn 3–5×/week.
- [ ] Ask your warm network for intros (target 20 asks).
- [ ] Goal: **10+ discovery calls booked.**
- [ ] Run free teardowns → convert to founding-client sprints.

### Weeks 7–10 — Convert + deliver + prove
- [ ] Close **2–3 founding clients** (discounted, see §7).
- [ ] Deliver fast and over-document. Capture metrics (time saved, before/after).
- [ ] Turn each into a written case study + testimonial + 2 referral asks.
- [ ] Keep outreach + content going (don't let pipeline dry up while delivering).

### Weeks 11–13 — Systematize
- [ ] Publish 2–3 case studies on the site (add a "Results"/"Case Studies" page).
- [ ] Raise prices to standard (drop founding discount).
- [ ] Lock in 1–2 referral partners.
- [ ] Review metrics (§9), double down on the channel that worked.

**90-day target: 3–5 paying clients, 2–3 case studies, 1 repeatable channel.**

## 7. The "Founding Client" play (your unfair advantage early)

Pitch your first 3–5 clients explicitly as founding clients:
- **30–50% off** the normal price (e.g., Setup Sprint at $1,500 instead of $2,500–5,000).
- In exchange: a **video testimonial + written case study + referral intros**.
- Frame it as exclusive and time-limited ("I'm taking on 3 founding clients this
  quarter at this rate").
- This solves the cold-start problem: you get revenue *and* the proof assets that
  make the next sale easier. Don't skip the testimonial/case-study trade — that's
  the whole point.

## 8. Outreach templates

> Rule: personalize the **first line** every time. Reference their company, a post,
> or a specific workflow. Keep emails under 120 words.

### Cold email — initial
**Subject:** quick idea for {Company}'s team

> Hi {First name},
>
> I noticed {specific trigger — e.g. "you're hiring 3 ops roles" / "your team
> ships a lot of client reports"}. Teams at that stage usually have AI tools like
> Claude or ChatGPT but haven't turned them into actual repeatable workflows — so
> the time savings never show up.
>
> That's all I do: install, customize, and train teams on AI workflows that stick.
>
> Want a free 30-min teardown? I'll map the 3 highest-ROI AI workflows for a team
> like yours — yours to keep whether or not we work together.
>
> Worth a quick look? — {Your name}, Novelle

### Cold email — follow-up (day 3, reply in thread)
> Hi {First name} — floating this back up. The teardown is genuinely free and
> takes 30 minutes. If now's not the time, just say "not now" and I'll stop. 🙂

### Cold email — value-add follow-up (day 7)
> {First name}, here's one your team could use today: {one concrete, specific
> workflow idea for their niche — e.g. "auto-draft first-pass client reports from
> a meeting transcript in Claude"}. Happy to map 2 more on a quick call. Want a slot?

### Cold email — break-up (day 12)
> Last one from me, {First name} — sounds like AI implementation isn't a priority
> right now, which is totally fair. If that changes, the free teardown offer
> stands. Wishing {Company} a strong quarter.

### LinkedIn connection note
> Hi {First name} — I help lean {niche} teams actually adopt AI (not just buy the
> subscription). Would love to connect.

### LinkedIn DM (after they accept)
> Thanks for connecting, {First name}! Quick one — is your team actually using
> Claude/ChatGPT day to day, or is it more "we have it but..."? I do free 20-min
> workflow teardowns for teams like {Company} if useful — no pitch, just value.

### Warm / referral ask
> Hey {Name} — I just launched Novelle; we get teams from "we have AI tools" to
> "AI is part of how we work." Do you know any 5–50 person teams that are
> AI-curious but stuck? I'm doing free teardowns for a few this month and would
> love an intro.

## 9. Metrics to track (weekly)

| Metric | Target (steady state) |
|--------|----------------------|
| Outbound touches / week | 100+ |
| Reply rate | 3–8% |
| Calls booked / week | 3–5 |
| Call → proposal rate | 50%+ |
| Proposal → close rate | 25–40% |
| LinkedIn posts / week | 3–5 |
| Cost per client (early) | ~$0 + your time |

Track these in your CRM. After 30 days, **kill what isn't working and double the
channel that books the most calls.** For most new agencies that's outbound + warm.
