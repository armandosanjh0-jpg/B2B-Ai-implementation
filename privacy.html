Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Fulcrum AI ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

1. Information we collect

When you submit our discovery call form or contact us, we collect the information you provide, which may include: your name, work email, company name and website, role, team size, and details about your AI goals, workflows, timeline, and budget. We may also collect basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use our website.

2. How we use your information

  • To assess fit and prepare for your discovery call.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide our services.
  • To send you relevant follow-up about your request.
  • To improve our website and offering.

We do not sell your personal information. We use it only for the purposes described here or as otherwise permitted by law.

3. Consent

By submitting a form or emailing us, you consent to our collection and use of your information for the purposes above. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, subject to legal and contractual restrictions.

4. Service providers

We use third-party tools to operate our website and intake process, including form handling (Web3Forms), scheduling (Calendly), and website hosting. These providers process data on our behalf and may store information outside Canada. We share only what is necessary for them to perform their function.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes it was collected for, or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Safeguards

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information against loss, theft, and unauthorized access. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under PIPEDA you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at dosa53@outlook.com.
