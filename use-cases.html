Use Cases

What this looks like for real businesses.

These are the workflow and growth implementations we most commonly build for professional services firms and small businesses across both service tracks.

Track 1: AI Workflow & Operations

Law Firms & Legal Services

AI-assisted contract review summaries, client intake drafts, research memos, and internal knowledge bases. Attorneys recover hours per week without compromising quality or judgment.

Accounting & CPA Practices

Automated client communications, financial summary drafts, compliance checklist workflows, and document organization so staff spends time on high-value client work, not repetitive formatting.

Consulting & Advisory Firms

Proposal generation, research summaries, meeting notes turned into action plans, and client deliverable templates. Every consultant on your team producing consistent, polished output, faster.

Operations & Admin Teams

Recurring report automation, email drafting, SOP documentation, and meeting summary workflows. Free your ops and admin staff from the repetitive tasks that pile up every single day.

Financial Advisors & Wealth Management

Client update letter drafts, portfolio summary templates, compliance document workflows, and internal research systems, implemented with the appropriate controls for regulated environments.

Real Estate Agencies

Listing description drafts, client email templates, offer comparison summaries, and market research workflows so agents spend more time with clients and less time writing.

Track 2: Growth & Digital Visibility

Lead Generation for Service Businesses

Full lead gen setups for law firms, accountants, clinics, and consultancies. Meta and Instagram ad campaigns with targeted audiences, sharp creative, and lead capture funnels that fill your calendar.

Websites for Professional Firms

Custom website builds that position your firm credibly and convert visitors into consultation requests. Built fast, mobile-first, and SEO-ready from day one.

Meta Ads for Retail & E-commerce

Facebook and Instagram campaigns for product-based businesses. Audience segmentation, creative testing, retargeting flows, and ongoing optimization to drive consistent sales.

Digital Presence for Restaurants & Hospitality

Websites, Google Business optimization, and local ad campaigns that drive reservations, foot traffic, and private event bookings, with content that keeps your brand looking fresh.

Health & Wellness Practices

Lead generation campaigns for gyms, clinics, therapists, and wellness studios. Targeted Meta ads, appointment-booking funnels, and follow-up sequences that turn interest into booked appointments.

Local Service Businesses

Full digital presence for contractors, cleaners, landscapers, and other service businesses. A professional website, Google and Meta ads, and lead capture systems that turn local searches into booked jobs.
