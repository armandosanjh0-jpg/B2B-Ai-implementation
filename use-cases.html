Use Cases

What AI implementation actually looks like in practice.

These are the workflow categories we most commonly implement for lean teams.

Engineering teams

Claude Code setup for code review, documentation generation, PR summaries, and internal tooling. Teams go from scattered AI use to a consistent development workflow.

Operations and admin

Workflow automation for recurring tasks — internal reports, meeting summaries, email drafts, SOP documentation — so ops teams spend less time on repetitive output.

Agencies and service businesses

Client deliverable workflows — briefs, proposals, research summaries, and first drafts — designed so the whole team produces consistent output faster.

Internal research and knowledge

Knowledge retrieval systems and internal research workflows so teams stop re-answering the same questions and start building on what they already know.

Documentation and onboarding

AI-assisted documentation systems — runbooks, onboarding guides, internal wikis — that stay updated without someone manually maintaining them.

Sales and business development

Outreach drafts, proposal generation, and research workflows for BD teams — so sales reps spend time on relationships, not writing the same emails repeatedly.
